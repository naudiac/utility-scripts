🚚 CB Radio & Highway Trucker Guide (Bank 3 — 27 MHz AM)

Unlike standard amateur transceivers, the BC75XLT covers 25–54 MHz and receives full 40-channel CB radio:

  • Channel 19 (27.185 MHz AM): The primary nationwide trucker channel. Heavy traffic on I-16, I-75, US-1, and US-280. Listen for speed traps, road debris, weigh station status, and traffic backups.
  • Channel 9 (27.065 MHz AM): Official emergency & traveler assistance channel monitored by REACT and volunteers.
  • Channel 14 (27.125 MHz AM) & 17 (27.165 MHz AM): Regional log truck and gravel hauler coordination across rural Georgia.
  • Channel 6 (27.025 MHz AM): The famous "Superbowl" high-power skip channel where operators across the country test long-range amplifiers.
