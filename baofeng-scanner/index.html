🛰️ ISS Overhead Alerts — Tune to CH 26 (145.800 MHz)

The International Space Station passes over Georgia several times daily. When it's overhead, tune to Channel 26 and you may hear crew voice communications or the onboard FM repeater.

Tip: Best passes are at dawn and dusk when the ISS is sunlit but the ground is dark — that's when you can see it as a bright moving star AND listen on 145.8 MHz simultaneously.

⌚ What's Active Right Now? Quick Tips

  • Morning (6-9 AM): Railroad dispatchers most active (CH 23-26). NOAA weather updates on the hour.
  • Daytime (9 AM-5 PM): Business bands busiest (CH 32-34) — hear store/warehouse workers. Ham repeater nets run various scheduled times.
  • Evening (5-10 PM): Ham radio nets are most active. Many clubs run weekly nets (check repeater descriptions).
  • Overnight: Marine Ch 16 can pick up distant signals via atmospheric ducting. Railroad never sleeps.
  • Severe Weather: Switch to NOAA WX channels (CH 5-11) and SKYWARN repeaters (CH 46 K4GAS, CH 69 WX4PCH, CH 87 WX4PTC).
