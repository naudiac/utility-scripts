📡 Antigravity Network Setup

Hey Dad! 👋

Two steps. Will takes over after that.
Just follow exactly what's below.

1

Install Termux

Open the Google Play Store on your Samsung phone and search for Termux — install it. It's free.

Then open Termux and copy-paste this first command:

pkg install -y curl
⚠ It may ask questions during install — just press Enter to accept defaults every time.
2

Run the Setup

Now paste this command. It connects your phone to Will's home network automatically — no passwords, no logins.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/naudiac/utility-scripts/main/daddy-setup/daddy_setup.sh | bash
⏳ Takes about 2-3 minutes to run. When you see ✅ SETUP COMPLETE — you're done! Keep Termux open in the background.
🎉

That's it!

Once you see the green checkmark in Termux,
Will will take over and handle everything else remotely.

You don't need to touch the TV or do anything else.
Just keep the phone plugged in and Termux open!
